[question]
How are paintings shipped?

[answer]
Smaller works ship flat, packed in archival board and rigid mailers. Larger paintings ship rolled in a sturdy tube, ready to be re-stretched by a local framer (I include instructions and a list of recommended framers on request). Every shipment is insured for full value and tracked end-to-end.

[question]
Where do you ship from and to?

[answer]
Ships from Canada. I ship worldwide with full insurance and tracking. Most pieces reach EU addresses in 3–7 days, North America in 7–14 days, and the rest of the world in 10–21 days. Customs duties are the buyer's responsibility for international orders.

[question]
What's your return policy?

[answer]
14 days from delivery, no questions asked. If a painting doesn't feel right in your space, return it in its original packaging and I'll refund in full once it arrives back safely. Return shipping is the buyer's responsibility.

[question]
Is the painting ready to hang?

[answer]
Stretched paintings arrive ready to hang, with hardware attached. Rolled paintings need to be re-stretched by a framer first. I can recommend trusted framers, or you can use your local framing studio — either way I'll include instructions for safe handling.

[question]
Can I commission a custom piece?

[answer]
Yes. Commissions begin with a conversation about scale, palette, and mood — not a specific reference image. Timeline is typically 6–10 weeks from deposit. Write to the studio to start the conversation.

[question]
How long until I receive a painting?

[answer]
Available paintings ship within 5 business days of payment. Add transit time (see shipping above). For commissions, expect 6–10 weeks from approved deposit.

[question]
Do you offer payment plans?

[answer]
I'm happy to discuss split payment for larger works — usually two or three instalments over up to 90 days. Write to the studio with the piece you're considering and we'll work something out.

[question]
How do I care for an acrylic painting?

[answer]
Hang away from direct sunlight and from heat sources. Dust gently with a soft, dry brush every few months. Avoid water or cleaning products on the surface. Acrylic paintings cured properly will last a lifetime if cared for simply.
