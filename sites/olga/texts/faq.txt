[question]
How are paintings shipped?

[answer]
Watercolors ship flat, never rolled. An unframed painting goes into an archival sleeve, between two rigid boards, in a flat mailer. A framed painting goes into a box with corner protectors, and the glass is taped so a break can't touch the paper. Acrylics are wrapped and boxed. Every shipment is insured for full value and tracked end-to-end.

[question]
Where do you ship from and to?

[answer]
From Thornhill, Ontario, Canada. I ship worldwide with full insurance and tracking. Most pieces reach EU addresses in 3–7 days, North America in 7–14 days, and the rest of the world in 10–21 days. Customs duties are the buyer's responsibility for international orders.

[question]
Is the frame included?

[answer]
Each listing says what you get: unframed, matted, or framed under glass. When it says framed, the frame is included in the price. The size on each painting is the painted image. The mat and the frame are larger, and the listing gives the outer size.

[question]
Is the painting ready to hang?

[answer]
Framed paintings arrive ready to hang, with hardware attached. Unframed watercolors need a mat and a frame. I can recommend a framer, or you can use your local framing studio.

[question]
What's your return policy?

[answer]
14 days from delivery, no questions asked. If a painting doesn't feel right in your space, return it in its original packaging and I'll refund in full once it arrives back safely. Return shipping is the buyer's responsibility. If a painting arrives damaged, send me photos of the parcel and the painting, and I'll refund in full or replace the frame.

[question]
How long until I receive a painting?

[answer]
Available paintings ship within 5 business days of payment. Add transit time (see shipping above). For commissions, expect 6–10 weeks from approved deposit.

[question]
Can I commission a custom piece?

[answer]
Yes. Commissions begin with a conversation about size, palette and mood. The timeline is usually 6–10 weeks from deposit. Write to me to start the conversation.

[question]
Do you offer payment plans?

[answer]
For larger works I'm happy to split the payment, usually into two or three instalments over up to 90 days. Write to me with the piece you're considering, and we'll work something out.

[question]
How do I care for a watercolor?

[answer]
Keep it framed under glass, away from direct sun, heat and damp walls. Don't let the paper touch the glass; the mat keeps it apart. Dust only the frame and the glass. A watercolor painted with lightfast pigments and kept like this will hold its color for a very long time.
